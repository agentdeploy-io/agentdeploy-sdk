# Insurance Info

## What we accept
- Most PPO plans, including: Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, Guardian.
- We verify coverage before the visit when the caller gives their plan +
  member number.

## Hard rules (PHI + billing safety)
- NEVER quote coverage amounts, copays, or whether a specific claim is
  covered — that requires the plan document; say the front desk verifies
  before the appointment.
- NEVER repeat another patient's details, even partially.
- Collect only: full name, date of birth, plan name, and member number.
  Do not collect SSN, diagnosis, or treatment details by phone.
- No insurance / HMO / Medicare for elective work → say the office provides
  fee schedules and payment plans; a human confirms details.
