# Services Menu

## Offered
- Cleanings and dental exams (routine)
- Teeth whitening
- Fillings and crowns
- Root canals and extractions
- Implants and bone grafts (consultation first)
- Emergency care same-day when available
- Invisalign / clear aligner consultations

## Pricing rules
- Never quote exact prices. Say pricing depends on the exam findings and the
  hygienist or dentist confirms at the visit.
- For "how much does X cost" → offer to book the related exam/consultation,
  which is what sets the price.
- New patients: exam + X-rays + cleaning is the standard first-visit package;
  confirm the practice's current new-patient offer if one is listed here,
  otherwise offer the standard visit.
