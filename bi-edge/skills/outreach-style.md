# Outreach Style

## Tone
- Direct, conversational, respectful
- No hype, no buzzwords, no "revolutionize"
- Speak like a trusted advisor, not a salesperson

## Cadence
- First email: day 0
- Follow-up 1: day 3
- Follow-up 2: day 7
- Final touch: day 14

## Link-First Policy
- Always lead with a single Calendly link
- No attachments on cold outreach
- No multi-link emails

## Subject Lines
- Short (max 7 words)
- Reference the recipient's business by name when possible
- Avoid spam triggers: free, discount, guarantee

## Reply Handling
- Interested: respond within 4 hours, offer Calendly
- Question: answer directly, then offer Calendly
- Not interested: acknowledge, mark as closed
- Opt-out: immediate permanent flag, confirm removal
