# Objection Handling

## Common Objections

### "We already have a CRM / tool for that"
Acknowledge their setup. Clarify that the agent complements existing tools by handling the intake and scheduling layer, not replacing their CRM.

### "We're not ready for AI"
Reframe: this isn't a big AI project. It's a simple intake agent that answers the phone/email and books appointments. No training needed on their end.

### "How much does it cost?"
Don't quote on first touch. Offer a short demo call to understand their workflow first. Price depends on volume.

### "Send me more info"
Send a one-pager link (not attachment). Follow up in 2 days with a specific question about their current intake process.

### "We handle everything in-house"
Ask how many hours per week they spend on phone/email intake and manual quoting. Position the agent as time recovery, not headcount replacement.

### "Is this a robot calling us?"
Disclose immediately: yes, the initial outreach is AI-assisted, but the demo and onboarding are with a real human.

## Rules
- Never argue
- Never badmouth a competitor
- One rebuttal per objection, then move to CTA or close gracefully
